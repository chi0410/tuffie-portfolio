## 2. 專案背景

**區塊標籤**：專案背景

**內文**（照原稿，此區無導言句）
- 中：我就職的 NUWA Robotics，是一間整合軟硬體的機器人產品公司。過去應用多聚焦於家庭陪伴與教育場景；隨著機器人應用普及，公司也將發展重心延伸至更多商務場域。
- 中：在企業轉型方向確立後，我負責官網轉型規劃，重新梳理其溝通目標與內容架構，讓官網從產品展示平台，進一步成為承載品牌定位、價值傳遞與市場拓展的重要渠道。
- EN ✍️：NUWA Robotics, where I work, is a robotics product company integrating hardware and software. Its applications once centred on home companionship and education; as robotics adoption grew, the company extended its focus into a wider range of business contexts.
- EN ✍️：Once the transformation's direction was set, I led the website's transformation planning — restructuring its communication goals and content architecture so the site could move beyond a product showcase to become a key channel carrying brand positioning, value delivery, and market expansion.

## 3. 優化目標與執行策略

**區塊標籤**：優化目標與執行策略

**導言**
- 中：轉型方向確立後，我該如何從龐大資訊中對齊目標與任務
- EN ✍️：Once the direction was set, how could I align goals and tasks within an overwhelming amount of information?

**內文**
- 中：隨著公司從 2C 轉向 2B，產品也由教育場域拓展至多元商務場景。為重新定義官網在企業轉型中的角色，我透過內部訪談與競品研究，盤點企業目標、業務需求、未來發展與現有資源，並將跨部門洞察轉化為官網優化策略，完成資訊架構與內容設計規劃。
- EN ✍️：As the company shifted from B2C to B2B, its products expanded from education into diverse business scenarios. To redefine the website's role in this transformation, I ran internal interviews and competitor research to map out business goals, sales needs, future direction, and existing resources — turning cross-team insights into a website optimisation strategy, and completing the information architecture and content design plan.

## 4. 拆解需求

**區塊標籤**：拆解需求

**導言**
- 中：我先以企業目標為基礎，定義出官網優化策略
- EN ✍️：Starting from the company's goals, I defined the website optimisation strategy

**內文**
- 中：在對齊分析結果與企業目標後，我發現官網目前提供的關鍵資訊，與客戶實際所需資訊之間存在明顯落差；尤其頁面目標也缺乏一致性，無法連貫地引導客戶判斷是否進一步導入。
- EN ✍️：After aligning the analysis with the company's goals, I found a clear gap between the key information the site provided and what customers actually needed — and page-level goals lacked consistency, so nothing coherently guided customers toward deciding whether to adopt.

## 5. 定義溝通任務

**區塊標籤**：定義溝通任務

**導言**
- 中：將企業目標，轉譯成客戶在意的價值
- EN ✍️：Translating business goals into the value customers actually care about

**內文**
- 中：我透過 User Story 定義關鍵客戶在瀏覽過程中想得到什麼、需要看到什麼，再進一步拆解為不同階段的頁面任務，將重要資訊轉化為可理解的企業敘事與價值傳達。
- EN ✍️：Using user stories, I defined what key customers want to get and need to see while browsing — then broke that into page-level tasks by stage, turning critical information into a business narrative and value proposition people can understand.

## 6. 規劃理解路徑

**區塊標籤**：規劃理解路徑

**導言**
- 中：我面臨的是該如何簡化大量分散的關鍵訊息
- EN ✍️：The challenge was how to simplify a large volume of scattered, critical information

**內文**
- 中：NUWA 提供的不只是機器人本身，還包含軟體服務、管理服務與技術能力。而我觀察到，這些關鍵資訊雖然重要，卻分散在不同頁面中，讓客戶難以有效理解整體服務生態。
- 中：因此，我以客戶瀏覽歷程為基礎，重新整理資訊閱讀節奏，先建立產品與服務認知，再逐步延伸至各項能力與導入脈絡，幫助客戶建立對 NUWA RaaS Suite 的心智模型與應用想像。
- EN ✍️：NUWA offers more than the robots themselves — it also provides software services, management services, and technical capabilities. I observed that while this information was critical, it was scattered across different pages, making it hard for customers to grasp the service ecosystem as a whole.
- EN ✍️：So I reorganised the reading rhythm around the customer's browsing journey — first establishing awareness of the products and services, then extending into individual capabilities and adoption context — helping customers build a mental model of NUWA RaaS Suite and imagine how it applies to them.

| 左上 | 現場執行服務任務與互動 | 服務執行 |
| 右上 | 建立服務流程的互動與形象 | 流程設計 |
| 左下 | 強化機器人的互動表現力 | 智慧技術 |
| 右下 | 遠端掌握團隊、機器人的運作 | 維運管理 |
| 中心 | — | NUWA RaaS Suite |

## 8. Key Outcomes

**區塊標籤**：Key Outcomes（照原稿使用英文）

- 中：從資訊入口到商務決策入口
