## 2. 我的路徑

### Visual Craft

- 中：從品牌與視覺設計出發，建立對美感、資訊傳達與品牌一致性的敏感度。
- 英：Starting from brand and visual design, I built a sharp sense for aesthetics, information delivery, and brand consistency.

### Business Thinking

- 中：在電商品牌的實務中，學習理解消費者需求、規劃資訊路徑，將美感轉化為有效的商業溝通。
- 英：Through hands-on e-commerce brand work, I learned to understand consumer needs, plan information paths, and turn aesthetics into effective business communication.

### Product Design

- 中：隨著設計環境持續改變，我開始進修產品設計，並發現它與過去的經驗緊密相連，同樣需要理解使用者、梳理資訊與設計流暢的體驗。於是，我將累積的視覺、行銷與商業理解，延伸到流程、介面與產品體驗設計。
- 英：As the design landscape kept evolving, I began studying product design — and found it deeply connected to what came before: understanding users, structuring information, and crafting fluent experiences. So I extended my accumulated visual, marketing, and business understanding into flows, interfaces, and product experience design.

## 3. Design Values

### 副標

- 中：三個陪我走到現在的核心價值，它們也成為我做設計時最重要的依據。
- 英：Three core values that have carried me this far — and remain my most important compass in design.

### 價值一：先理解，再設計｜Understand First, Then Design

- 中：好的設計始於理解脈絡。梳理情境、角色、流程與條件，建立完整而清晰的訊息。
- 英：Good design starts with understanding context. By mapping scenarios, roles, flows, and constraints, I build a complete and clear picture.

### 價值二：解開打結的毛線團｜Untangling the Knots

- 中：把複雜的問題理順。透過架構、流程與系統化思考，將打結的部分轉化成清晰、好理解的資訊。
- 英：Making complex problems make sense. Through structure, flows, and systems thinking, I turn the tangled parts into clear, understandable information.

### 價值三：融入生活中的設計｜Design That Blends into Life

- 中：設計最美的時刻，是自然地被使用，不被察覺，在理想與限制之間協調。既能落地，同時默默創造價值。
- 英：Design is at its best when it's used naturally, without being noticed — balancing ideals and constraints, grounded in reality while quietly creating value.
