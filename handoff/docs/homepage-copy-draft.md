## 2. About Me

### 區塊標題

Sense and Sensibility in Design

### 精簡自介

**中文版**

我相信產品設計不只是建立嚴謹的邏輯與規格，同時需要沈浸在理解使用者體驗中的愉悅感。唯有在理性判斷中注入共情，產品才能被理解、被使用，並持續創造價值。

我是一位擅長梳理問題，將複雜的系統架構、邏輯與操作規則，轉譯為清晰且可落地體驗方案的產品設計師。

擁有七年品牌視覺設計經驗，近三年專注於 UI/UX 設計領域。目前於女媧創造 NUWA Robotics，參與機器人與 SaaS 整合產品的介面、流程及跨裝置體驗設計，具備 AI 機器人產品從規劃到上線的實務經驗。

**English**

I believe product design is more than building rigorous logic and specifications — it also means immersing yourself in the joy of understanding how people experience a product. Only when empathy is woven into rational judgment can a product be understood, be used, and keep creating value.

I'm a product designer who untangles problems — translating complex system architectures, logic, and operational rules into clear, actionable experience solutions.

With seven years in brand visual design and the past three focused on UI/UX, I now work at NUWA Robotics on the interfaces, workflows, and cross-device experiences of robot–SaaS integrated products, with hands-on experience taking AI robot products from planning to launch.
